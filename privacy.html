LogPet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. What data we collect

LogPet collects the following data to provide the pet care tracking service:

  • Account information: email address, display name (from Apple Sign In or Google Sign In)
  • Pet data: pet names, species, breed, birth date, photos, weight, and other profile details you provide
  • Activity records: feeding times, walks, medications, and other care activities you log
  • Care schedules: medication plans, reminders, and related notes

2. How we use your data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Provide the pet care tracking functionality
  • Sync data across your devices and with care space members you invite
  • Display charts, insights, and streaks based on your records
  • Send local notifications for care reminders (on-device only)

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising or analytics purposes.

3. Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure). All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Database access is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only access their own data and data shared within their care spaces.

Pet photos are stored in Supabase Storage with access controls.

4. Data sharing

Your data is shared only with members of care spaces you create or join. You control who has access through the invite system and role assignments (owner, admin, editor, viewer).

We do not share your data with any third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can:

  • Export your data at any time (CSV or JSON) from Settings
  • Delete your account and all associated data permanently from Settings > "Delete account and all data"

Account deletion removes all your pets, activity records, care schedules, care space memberships, and profile data immediately and permanently.

6. Third-party services

  • Apple Sign In — authentication only, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign In — authentication only, governed by Google's Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — data hosting, governed by Supabase Privacy Policy

7. Children's privacy

LogPet is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
